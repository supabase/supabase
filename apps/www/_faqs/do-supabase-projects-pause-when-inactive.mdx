---
title: "Do Supabase projects pause when they're inactive?"
description: 'Only on the Free plan, after 7 days of low database activity. Projects on paid plans never pause, and a paused Free project can be restored from the dashboard.'
date: '2026-09-28'
---

Only Free plan projects pause, and only after a week of low database activity. Projects on the Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans are never paused for inactivity.

## How pausing works on the Free plan

Supabase looks at database activity over the past 7 days. A Free project that gets too few user queries in that window becomes a candidate for pausing. A few requests to the database each day is usually enough to keep it running.

You get two emails:

1. A warning roughly a week before the pause takes effect.
2. A confirmation once the project is paused.

Paused projects don't count toward the Free plan's limit of 2 active projects.

## What happens to your data

Nothing is deleted. A paused project keeps its data and configuration. To bring it back:

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard) and select the organization.
2. Select the paused project.
3. Click **Resume project** and confirm.

You can restore a paused project for up to 1 year after it was paused.

## How to avoid pausing

- **Upgrade the organization to a paid plan.** Paid projects can't be paused and aren't subject to inactivity pausing. The Pro plan starts at $25 per month and includes one project on Micro compute. See [How much does a single database-only Supabase project cost?](/faqs/how-much-does-a-database-only-supabase-project-cost)
- **Keep real traffic flowing.** A project that your app queries every day stays active.

For the full rules, see [Project pausing](/docs/guides/platform/free-project-pausing).

## When this matters

Pausing is designed for projects that are genuinely idle, such as experiments and tutorials. If you're deploying something that people rely on, even with low traffic, put it on a paid plan so it's always reachable.
