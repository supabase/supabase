title: 'How much does a single database-only Supabase project cost?'
description: 'A database-only project is free on the Free plan with a 500 MB database, or $25 per month on Pro, which includes one project on Micro compute and 8 GB of disk.'
date: '2026-09-28'
---

On the Free plan it costs nothing. On the Pro plan, a single project on Micro compute costs $25 per month before any usage beyond the included quotas.

## Free plan

The Free plan is $0 per month and includes:

- A 500 MB database on shared CPU with 500 MB of RAM
- 5 GB of egress
- Up to 2 active projects
- Community support

Free projects pause after 7 days of low activity. See [Do Supabase projects pause when they're inactive?](/faqs/do-supabase-projects-pause-when-inactive)

## Pro plan

The Pro plan starts at $25 per month and includes:

- One project on Micro compute, through $10 per month in compute credits
- 8 GB of disk per project, then $0.125 per GB
- 250 GB of egress
- Daily backups kept for 7 days
- Email support

Micro compute has 1 GB of RAM. Projects on Pro never pause.

## What changes the bill

- **More projects.** Each project runs on its own compute instance, billed hourly. The compute credits cover one Micro instance, so a second Micro project adds its compute cost.
- **Bigger compute.** Larger instances cost more per hour. See [Compute and disk](/docs/guides/platform/compute-and-disk).
- **More disk or egress** beyond the included amounts.
- **Add-ons** such as Point-in-Time Recovery or a dedicated IPv4 address.

You're billed at the organization level. For how plans, compute, and credits combine, see [Billing on Supabase](/docs/guides/platform/billing-on-supabase) and the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Using only the database

You don't need to use Auth, Storage, Realtime, or Edge Functions to use the database. Connect with a standard Postgres connection string from any client or ORM. See [Can I use Supabase as just a Postgres database?](/faqs/can-i-use-supabase-as-just-a-postgres-database)
