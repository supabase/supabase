## 1. Germany: Professional Secrecy (Section 203 of the German Criminal Code).

### a. Scope

This Section 1 applies only to a Customer that (i) is a person or entity described in Section 203(1) or Section 203(2) of the German Criminal Code (Strafgesetzbuch, "StGB") and (ii) has notified Supabase of that status by submitting the form available at [https://supabase.link/germanyprofessionalsecrecy](https://supabase.link/germanyprofessionalsecrecy), identifying the Customer account or organization to which the notice relates (a "Professional Secrecy Customer"). This Section 1 applies only to Customer Data of a Professional Secrecy Customer that constitutes a third-party secret within the meaning of Section 203 StGB and that the Professional Secrecy Customer submits to the Services ("Protected Secrets"). It does not apply to any other Customer or to any other Customer Data. Capitalized terms not defined in this Section 1 have the meanings given in the Terms of Service or the Data Processing Addendum.

### b. Customer Responsibilities

Consistent with Section 3.c of the Terms of Service, the Professional Secrecy Customer is solely responsible for (i) determining whether it is subject to Section 203 StGB; (ii) determining whether its disclosure of Protected Secrets to Supabase and its use of the Services is permitted under Section 203 StGB and any professional rules applicable to it, including any client consent, location of processing, or other requirement imposed on the Professional Secrecy Customer by those rules; and (iii) limiting its disclosure of Protected Secrets to Supabase to what is necessary for its use of the Services. The Professional Secrecy Customer represents that the information in its notice under Section 1.a is accurate. Supabase has no obligation to identify or monitor Protected Secrets within Customer Data.

### c. Supabase Undertakings

With respect to Protected Secrets, Supabase will: (i) treat Protected Secrets as Customer's Confidential Information under Section 6 of the Terms of Service, and use and disclose Protected Secrets only as necessary to provide the Services to the Professional Secrecy Customer in accordance with the Agreement and the Data Processing Addendum; (ii) limit access to Protected Secrets in accordance with Section 5 of the Data Processing Addendum and Schedule 1 (Technical and Organizational Measures) to the Data Processing Addendum; (iii) ensure that each of its personnel and each Authorized Sub-processor that may access Protected Secrets is bound in text form to confidentiality obligations covering Protected Secrets, in accordance with Sections 5 and 6.2 of the Data Processing Addendum; and (iv) require each Authorized Sub-processor that may access Protected Secrets to bind, in text form, any further person it engages that may access Protected Secrets to equivalent confidentiality obligations.

The Professional Secrecy Customer informs Supabase, and Supabase acknowledges, that a person who participates in the professional activity of a Professional Secrecy Customer and who, without authorization, discloses a third-party secret learned in that capacity may be subject to criminal penalties under Section 203(4) StGB.
