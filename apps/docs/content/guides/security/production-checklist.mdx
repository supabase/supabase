Secure your project before you launch it. Work through each group below, and check your database for issues with [Security Advisor](/dashboard/project/_/database/security-advisor). For performance and availability checks, see the [Production checklist](/docs/guides/deployment/going-into-prod).

## Database

- Enable Row Level Security (RLS) on every table in the [**Database > Tables**](/dashboard/project/_/database/tables) section of the Dashboard. A table without RLS and reasonable policies lets any client read and change its data. To learn more, see [Row Level Security](/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security).
- Enable replication on tables containing sensitive data by enabling RLS and setting row security policies:
  - Go to the [**Database > Policies**](/dashboard/project/_/database/policies) section of the Dashboard to enable RLS and create policies.
  - Go to the [**Database > Publications**](/dashboard/project/_/database/publications) section of the Dashboard to manage replicated tables.
- Turn on [SSL Enforcement](/docs/guides/platform/ssl-enforcement) in the [**Database > Settings > SSL Configuration**](/dashboard/project/_/database/settings#ssl-configuration) section of the Dashboard.
- Enable [Network Restrictions](/docs/guides/platform/network-restrictions) in the [**Database > Settings > Network Restrictions**](/dashboard/project/_/database/settings#network-restrictions) section of the Dashboard.

## Account and organization

- Protect your Supabase account with multi-factor authentication (MFA):
  - If you sign in with GitHub, [enable 2FA on GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/securing-your-account-with-two-factor-authentication-2fa/configuring-two-factor-authentication). Your GitHub account gives you administrative rights to your Supabase organization, so protect it with a strong password and 2FA, using a U2F key or a TOTP app.
  - If you sign in with an email and password, set up [MFA for your Supabase account](/docs/guides/platform/multi-factor-authentication#enable-mfa).
- Consider enabling [MFA enforcement on your organization](/docs/guides/platform/mfa/org-mfa-enforcement). Every member then needs a valid MFA-backed session to work with organization and project resources.
- Consider adding more than one owner to your organization in the [**Organization > Team**](/dashboard/org/_/team) section of the Dashboard. If one owner is unreachable or loses access to their account, you still have Owner access to your organization.

## Auth

- Enable email confirmations in the [**Authentication > Providers**](/dashboard/project/_/auth/providers) section of the Dashboard.
- Remove any [test phone numbers](/docs/guides/auth/testing-auth-flows#test-phone-sign-in-with-test-numbers) you added for testing.
- Set the expiry for one-time passwords (OTPs) in the [**Authentication > Providers**](/dashboard/project/_/auth/providers) section of the Dashboard. Use 3600 seconds (1 hour) or lower. If you need more entropy, increase the length of the OTP.
- If your application needs a higher level of security, set up [multi-factor authentication](/docs/guides/auth/auth-mfa) for your users.
- Use a custom SMTP server for Auth emails, so your users see mail from a trusted domain, ideally the domain your app runs on. Get SMTP credentials from any major email provider, such as SendGrid or AWS SES.

## Threat review

- Consider how _you_ might abuse your service as an attacker, and take steps to mitigate it.
- Review these [common cybersecurity threats](https://auth0.com/docs/security/prevent-threats).
