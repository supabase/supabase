Transfer a project to another organization to change who pays for it, to hand it to a client, or to move it to a different plan. A transfer changes the project's organization. The project keeps its data, URL, and API keys.

The _source organization_ is the organization the project belongs to before the transfer. The _target organization_ is the organization you move the project to.

- [Check the requirements](#check-the-requirements) lists what you need before you start, and what blocks a transfer.
- [Transfer a project](#transfer-a-project) has the steps for the Supabase Dashboard and the Management API.
- [What changes after a transfer](#what-changes-after-a-transfer) covers data, downtime, billing, access, and notifications.
- [Transfer to a Free Plan organization](#transfer-to-a-free-plan-organization) lists what the Free Plan removes, and how to pause a project.
- [Other transfer scenarios](#other-transfer-scenarios) covers handing a project to another person, lost access, partner-managed projects, and region changes.
- [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting) maps each Dashboard message to its cause and fix.

## Check the requirements

The Supabase Dashboard checks every requirement when you select a target organization. **Transfer Project** stays disabled until all of them pass.

Your account must meet the following requirements:

- You're an **Owner** of the source organization. An Administrator can't transfer projects.
- You're a member of the target organization with a role for the whole organization: Owner, Administrator, or Developer. On the Team Plan and Enterprise Plan, the Read-only role also qualifies. The **No access** role and project-scoped roles don't qualify. For more information about roles, see [Access control](/docs/guides/platform/access-control).
- You use the same account in both organizations. An account that signs in with single sign-on (SSO) and a non-SSO account with the same email address are separate accounts, so a role held by one doesn't count for the other. If your SSO account isn't a member of both organizations, use a non-SSO account that is. For more information, see [Enable SSO for your organization](/docs/guides/platform/sso).

The project and the source organization must meet the following requirements:

- The project has no GitHub connection. Disconnect the [GitHub integration](/docs/guides/deployment/branching/github-integration), then reconnect it after the transfer.
- No member has a project-scoped role on the project. Remove those roles before the transfer. To assign them again afterwards, the target organization must be on the Team Plan or Enterprise Plan.
- If both organizations are on the Free Plan, the source organization has no usage restriction. See the [Usage restrictions on Free Plan organizations](#usage-restrictions-on-free-plan-organizations) section.
- If the source organization is on the Free Plan or Pro Plan, you have no overdue invoices. See the [Overdue invoices](#overdue-invoices) section.
- The project doesn't use a feature that the target organization lacks. See the [Features that block a transfer](#features-that-block-a-transfer) section.

### Usage restrictions on Free Plan organizations

A Free Plan organization that exceeded its quota can't transfer projects to another Free Plan organization. The Dashboard shows the following message:

```text
This project can't be transferred to a Free organization while the source organization is in an active grace period or under service restrictions. Resolve the restriction or upgrade the source organization before transferring.
```

The block applies only when both organizations are on the Free Plan. It stays in place in all of the following cases:

- Your usage drops back under the quota.
- The grace period ends.
- The restriction on the organization is lifted.

The block ends when the restriction notice clears from the source organization. The notice clears automatically if you continue to stay under plan limits for multiple billing cycles. For more information, see [What is a grace period and does it reset after usage drops?](/docs/guides/platform/billing-faq#what-is-a-grace-period-and-does-it-reset-after-usage-drops).

To transfer without waiting, use either of these options:

- Upgrade the source organization to the Pro Plan. The upgrade lifts the restriction. The plan fee applies to the whole organization. For the cost, see [Pricing](/pricing).
- Transfer the project to an organization on the Pro Plan and above.

Supabase support can't lift a restriction to allow a transfer.

If you upgrade the source organization only to transfer a project, transfer the project before you downgrade. An organization that returns to the Free Plan can be restricted again.

### Overdue invoices

Overdue invoices block a transfer to any target organization when the source organization is on the Free Plan or the Pro Plan. The check covers the source organization and every other Free Plan or Pro Plan organization where you're an Owner or Administrator. The Dashboard names the organizations in the following message:

```text
There are overdue invoices in the organization(s) <organization names>. Head to the organization's invoices page to settle the invoices before transferring the project.
```

Pay the overdue invoices on each organization's [invoices page](/dashboard/org/_/billing#invoices). The check also passes when an organization's credit balance covers its overdue amount.

### Features that block a transfer

Some features block a transfer until you remove them or choose a different target organization. The following table lists each feature, when it blocks a transfer, and what to do.

| Feature                                                                                               | Blocks the transfer when                                                    | What to do                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Preview and persistent branches](/docs/guides/deployment/branching)                                  | The target organization is on the Free Plan                                 | Delete the branches                                                   |
| [Log drains](/docs/guides/observability/log-drains)                                                   | The target organization is on the Free Plan                                 | Remove the log drains                                                 |
| [Advanced multi-factor authentication (MFA)](/docs/guides/auth/auth-mfa/phone) with phone or WebAuthn | The target organization is on the Free Plan                                 | Turn off phone and WebAuthn MFA                                       |
| [Pipelines](/docs/guides/database/replication/pipelines)                                              | The target organization is on the Free Plan                                 | Delete all pipelines                                                  |
| High availability                                                                                     | The target organization has no high availability access or capacity         | Choose a target organization with high availability access            |
| [AWS PrivateLink](/docs/guides/platform/privatelink)                                                  | The project has a PrivateLink association                                   | Remove the PrivateLink associations                                   |
| [Vector buckets](/docs/guides/storage/vector/creating-vector-buckets)                                 | The source organization has access to vector buckets and the target doesn't | Contact support to turn on vector buckets for the target organization |
| [Stripe Projects](/docs/guides/integrations/stripe-projects)                                          | The project was provisioned through Stripe                                  | See [Stripe Projects](#stripe-projects)                               |
| A restore in progress                                                                                 | The project is restoring                                                    | Wait until the restore finishes                                       |

Compliance settings don't block a transfer. If your project is a HIPAA project, transfer it only to an organization that also has the HIPAA add-on. See the [shared responsibility model](/docs/guides/deployment/shared-responsibility-model).

## Transfer a project

<Admonition type="caution">

A transfer to a Free Plan organization removes add-ons, and transferring the project back doesn't restore them. A transfer has no undo. To move a project back, transfer it again, which runs the same checks.

</Admonition>

Transfer one project at a time. To transfer several projects, repeat the steps for each project. The Supabase CLI has no transfer command.

<Tabs scrollable size="small" type="underlined" defaultActiveId="dashboard" queryGroup="method">

1. Open the project's [general settings](/dashboard/project/_/settings/general).
2. In the **Transfer project** section, click **Transfer project**.
3. In **Select Target Organization**, select the organization to move the project to.
4. Read the result of the checks.
5. Click **Transfer Project**.

The checks run when you select the target organization. A **Project cannot be transferred** box lists what blocks the transfer. Warnings list what the target organization's plan changes or removes.

<TabPanel id="api" label="Management API">

1. Create an [access token](/dashboard/account/tokens). For a scoped token, under **Resource access**, select **Organization**, choose both the source and the target organization, and grant the following permissions. A token with **Project** access can't preview or transfer a project. For more information, see [Personal access tokens](/docs/guides/platform/personal-access-tokens).
   - **Project Settings** with **Read** access, for the preview
   - **Organization Settings** with **Read-write** access, for the transfer
   - **Organization Members** with **Read** access, for the check on the target organization
2. Set the token, the project ref, and the target organization's slug:

   ```bash
   export SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN="your-access-token"
   export PROJECT_REF="your-project-ref"
   export TARGET_ORG_SLUG="target-organization-slug"
   ```

3. Preview the transfer:

   ```bash
   curl -X POST "https://api.supabase.com/v2/projects/$PROJECT_REF/transfers/previews" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
     -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d @- <<EOF
   {
     "data": {
       "type": "project_transfer_input",
       "attributes": { "target_organization_slug": "$TARGET_ORG_SLUG" }
     }
   }
   EOF
   ```

   The response has a `valid` field under `data.attributes`, next to lists of `errors`, `warnings`, and `info` messages. Continue when `valid` is `true`.

4. Transfer the project:

   ```bash
   curl -X POST "https://api.supabase.com/v2/projects/$PROJECT_REF/transfers" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
     -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d @- <<EOF
   {
     "data": {
       "type": "project_transfer_input",
       "attributes": { "target_organization_slug": "$TARGET_ORG_SLUG" }
     }
   }
   EOF
   ```

   A successful transfer returns `200` with an empty body.

For the full request and response schemas, see [Preview a project transfer](/docs/reference/api/v2-preview-a-project-transfer) and [Transfer a project](/docs/reference/api/v2-transfer-a-project) in the Management API reference.

</TabPanel>
</Tabs>

The project now belongs to the target organization.

## What changes after a transfer

A transfer between two organizations on the same plan changes only the project's organization. A transfer to a different plan also applies the target plan's features and limits.

### What stays the same

A transfer doesn't copy or move data. The project stays on the same infrastructure, and the following stay as they are:

- The database
- Auth users and sessions
- Storage buckets and files
- Edge Functions and their secrets
- Cron jobs
- Realtime

The following identifiers also stay the same, so your apps keep working without changes:

- The project ref and API URL
- The API keys, including the legacy `anon` and `service_role` keys
- The JWT secret and signing keys
- The database connection strings and the pooler host

Read replicas and branches move with the project when the target organization is on the Pro Plan and above.

Update your apps or tools in the following cases:

- The target organization is on the Free Plan, and the project used a feature that the Free Plan removes. Examples are a custom domain, the IPv4 add-on, and SAML sign-in. See [Transfer to a Free Plan organization](#transfer-to-a-free-plan-organization).
- You use an access token scoped to the source organization, or an app that you authorized for the source organization. See [Access and tokens](#access-and-tokens).
- The project was in a Vercel-managed organization. See [Vercel Marketplace](#vercel-marketplace).

### Downtime

A transfer restarts the database only when it changes the project's compute size. That happens when you move a project on compute larger than Nano to a Free Plan organization. The following table lists the database downtime for each direction.

| Transfer                                                           | Downtime                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Between two organizations on the same plan                         | None                                               |
| From the Free Plan to the Pro Plan and above                       | None                                               |
| Between two different plans, Pro Plan and above                    | None                                               |
| From the Pro Plan and above to the Free Plan, compute above Nano   | About a minute, while the project restarts on Nano |
| From the Pro Plan and above to the Free Plan, compute already Nano | None                                               |

The restart is queued when you confirm the transfer, and usually begins within seconds.

A transfer to a Free Plan organization also restarts other services:

- The Auth service restarts to apply the Free Plan settings, even when the compute size doesn't change.
- Removing a custom domain or vanity subdomain restarts the Auth service and the API gateway. Requests to the custom domain stop working.
- Removing the IPv4 add-on doesn't restart the project, but connections to the IPv4 address stop working.

For the full list, see the [What the transfer removes](#what-the-transfer-removes) section.

A project that moves from the Free Plan to the Pro Plan and above keeps its Nano compute. You can upgrade it to Micro at no extra cost. See [Compute and disk](/docs/guides/platform/compute-and-disk).

### Billing

The source organization pays for the project's usage and add-ons up to the transfer. The target organization pays for them after the transfer. The charges appear on each organization's invoice when its billing cycle resets.

Usage includes metrics such as disk size, [egress](/kb/troubleshooting/all-about-supabase-egress-a_Sg_e), and image transformations. Add-ons include [compute](/docs/guides/platform/compute-and-disk), [Point-in-Time Recovery](/docs/guides/platform/backups#point-in-time-recovery), [IPv4](/docs/guides/platform/ipv4-address), and [custom domains](/docs/guides/platform/custom-domains).

The following stay with each organization:

- **Subscription and plan fee.** The transfer doesn't change or prorate either organization's subscription.
- **Credit balance.** Credits stay with the source organization. To move credits to another organization, [contact support](https://supabase.help).
- **Compute Credits.** Each organization's Compute Credits apply to its own compute hours. They cover one project on Nano or Micro compute, so every further project adds compute costs. See [Manage Compute usage](/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/compute).
- **Payment method and billing details.** The target organization pays with its own payment method. The payment method, billing address, and billing email never move with a project.

When you move a Free Plan project to an organization on the Pro Plan and above that already has active projects, the Dashboard shows the added monthly compute cost before you confirm.

### Access and tokens

A transfer doesn't change anyone's membership or role. Your role in the target organization applies to the project after the transfer. For example, an Owner of the source organization who has the Developer role in the target organization gets Developer access to the project. Members of the source organization who aren't members of the target organization lose access to the project.

Personal access tokens for the Management API behave differently depending on their scope. The following table lists each case.

| Token                                                    | After the transfer                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Token with your account's full access, marked **Legacy** | Keeps working, if you're a member of the target organization                    |
| Token scoped to the source organization                  | Stops working for the project. Create a token scoped to the target organization |
| Token scoped to the target organization                  | Starts working for the project                                                  |
| Token scoped to the project                              | Keeps working, if you still have a role on the project                          |

You might need to re-authorize connected apps for the target organization. That includes OAuth apps and Model Context Protocol (MCP) clients that you authorized for the source organization.

### Timing and notifications

The project moves to the target organization as soon as you confirm. Changes that come from the target plan, such as a compute change, finish within minutes.

Supabase doesn't send an email about a transfer. Organizations with access to [Platform Webhooks](/docs/guides/platform/webhooks), an early access feature, can subscribe to the [`v1.project.transferred`](/docs/guides/platform/webhooks/events#v1projecttransferred) event, which fires for both organizations.

The transfer appears in your [account audit log](/dashboard/account/audit). On the Team Plan and Enterprise Plan, it also appears in the source organization's [audit log](/docs/guides/security/platform-audit-logs). Activity after the transfer appears in the target organization's audit log.

## Transfer to a Free Plan organization

A project that moves to a Free Plan organization loses the features that the Free Plan doesn't include. Usage above the Free Plan quota can also lead to restrictions, so compare the project's usage with the Free Plan limits before you transfer.

### What the transfer removes

The Dashboard lists the affected add-ons before you confirm. The transfer removes or changes the following automatically:

| Item                               | What happens                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Compute                            | Changes to Nano. A project on larger compute restarts. See the [Downtime](#downtime) section.                                                                                 |
| Point-in-Time Recovery             | The add-on is removed.                                                                                                                                                        |
| Backups                            | The **Backups** page no longer lists backups, because the Free Plan doesn't include them.                                                                                     |
| Read replicas                      | Removed.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Custom domain and vanity subdomain | Removed. Point your clients back to the project's default `supabase.co` URL.                                                                                                  |
| IPv4 add-on                        | Removed. Direct connections become IPv6 only. On an IPv4-only network, connect through the [shared pooler](/docs/guides/database/connecting-to-postgres#pooler-session-mode). |
| Branching                          | Turned off for the project.                                                                                                                                                   |
| Auth features                      | SAML sign-in, session time-boxing, the session inactivity timeout, and leaked password protection are turned off.                                                             |
| Email templates                    | Reset to the defaults for some projects that send email through the default email provider. Projects with custom SMTP or a Send Email hook keep their templates.              |
| Realtime settings                  | Settings above the Free Plan limits are reset.                                                                                                                                |
| Storage features                   | Image transformations, analytics buckets, and CDN cache purging are turned off. The global file size limit returns to the Free Plan limit.                                    |

The transfer doesn't remove branches, log drains, Advanced MFA, or Pipelines. Remove them yourself first, because each one blocks a transfer to the Free Plan. See [Features that block a transfer](#features-that-block-a-transfer).

### Free project limit

The free project limit is counted for each member, not for each organization. Every Owner and Administrator of the target organization can have two active Free Plan projects, counted across all Free Plan organizations they own or administer. Paused projects don't count.

When the transfer would put a member over the limit, the Dashboard lists the affected members. To continue, use one of these options:

- Ask each listed member to pause or delete one of their Free Plan projects, or to upgrade one of their Free Plan organizations.
- Change the member's role to Developer in the target organization's [team settings](/dashboard/org/_/team). The limit counts only Owners and Administrators.
- Transfer the project to an organization on the Pro Plan and above.

### Pause a project from the Pro Plan or Team Plan

Projects in an organization on the Pro Plan or Team Plan can't be paused. To pause one:

1. Transfer the project to a Free Plan organization where you're an Owner or Administrator. Other roles can't pause a project.
2. Pause the project in its [general settings](/dashboard/project/_/settings/general), or with the Management API's [pause a project](/docs/reference/api/v1-pause-a-project) endpoint.

Before you use this route, check the following:

- The transfer removes add-ons and features that the Free Plan doesn't include, and a second transfer doesn't restore them. See the [What the transfer removes](#what-the-transfer-removes) section.
- The transfer runs every check in the [Check the requirements](#check-the-requirements) section, including overdue invoices and the free project limit.
- On the Free Plan, you can resume a paused project only if its database is 1 GB or smaller. To resume a larger project, first transfer the paused project to an organization on the Pro Plan and above.

You can transfer a project that's already paused. It stays paused after the transfer. For more information about pausing and the time limit for resuming, see [Project pausing](/docs/guides/platform/free-project-pausing).

## Other transfer scenarios

The following sections cover handing a project to someone else, lost access, partner-managed projects, and region changes.

### Give a project to another person

Projects belong to organizations, not to user accounts. To give a project to another person or a client, or to move it to a new account of your own, move the project to their organization or hand over your whole organization.

<Admonition type="caution">

Leaving an organization permanently deletes the content you saved in its projects: SQL snippets, including shared ones, custom reports, and Log Explorer queries. Copy anything that you or the recipient need before you leave.

</Admonition>

#### Option 1: Move the project to their organization

1. Ask the recipient to create an organization and invite you with a role for the whole organization. The Developer role is enough. On the Team Plan or Enterprise Plan, the recipient can also send the invitation with the [create organization invitations](/docs/reference/api/v2-create-organization-invitations) endpoint.
2. Accept the invitation from the invitation email. The Management API has no endpoint for accepting an invitation.
3. [Transfer the project](#transfer-a-project) to their organization, with the Dashboard or the Management API steps in that section.
4. In their organization's [team settings](/dashboard/org/_/team), click **Leave team**. The recipient can also remove you, from the same page or with the [remove organization members](/docs/reference/api/v2-remove-organization-members) endpoint.

#### Option 2: Hand over the whole organization

You can't add an Owner from Supabase to an organization managed through the [Vercel Marketplace](/docs/guides/integrations/vercel-marketplace). Give the person the `owner` role on your Vercel team instead.

<Tabs scrollable size="small" type="underlined" defaultActiveId="dashboard" queryGroup="method">
<TabPanel id="dashboard" label="Dashboard">

1. Invite the recipient to your organization as an **Owner**, or promote a member to **Owner**.
2. After the recipient accepts, ask them to add their payment method in the organization's [billing settings](/dashboard/org/_/billing) and to select **Use this card**. Then delete your card. You can't delete the card that's active. A payment method that stays with the organization can still be charged after you leave.
3. In the organization's [team settings](/dashboard/org/_/team), click **Leave team**.

</TabPanel>
<TabPanel id="api" label="Management API">

1. Create an [access token](/dashboard/account/tokens). For a scoped token, under **Resource access**, select **Organization**, choose the organization, and grant **Organization Members** with **Read-write** access and **Organization Settings** with **Read-write** access.
2. Set the token, the organization's slug, and the recipient's email address:

   ```bash
   export SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN="your-access-token"
   export ORG_SLUG="your-organization-slug"
   export RECIPIENT_EMAIL="recipient@example.com"
   ```

3. Make the recipient an Owner. Both of the following requests need the Team Plan or Enterprise Plan. On other plans, use the Dashboard for this step.

   If the recipient isn't a member of the organization, invite them with the [create organization invitations](/docs/reference/api/v2-create-organization-invitations) endpoint:

   ```bash
   curl -X POST "https://api.supabase.com/v2/organizations/$ORG_SLUG/members/invitations" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
     -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d @- <<EOF
   {
     "data": [
       {
         "type": "organization_invitation",
         "attributes": { "email": "$RECIPIENT_EMAIL", "role": "owner" }
       }
     ]
   }
   EOF
   ```

   If the recipient is already a member, find their `id` with the [list organization members](/docs/reference/api/v2-list-organization-members) endpoint. Filter by their email address, because the endpoint returns one page of members at a time:

   ```bash
   curl -g "https://api.supabase.com/v2/organizations/$ORG_SLUG/members?filter[primary_email]=$RECIPIENT_EMAIL" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN"
   ```

   Copy the `id` from the response. Then change their role with the [assign organization member role](/docs/reference/api/v2-assign-organization-member-role) endpoint:

   ```bash
   export MEMBER_ID="recipient-member-id"

   curl -X PATCH "https://api.supabase.com/v2/organizations/$ORG_SLUG/members/$MEMBER_ID/roles" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
     -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d @- <<EOF
   {
     "data": {
       "type": "organization_member_role",
       "attributes": { "role": "owner" }
     }
   }
   EOF
   ```

4. After the recipient is an Owner, ask them to add their payment method in the organization's [billing settings](/dashboard/org/_/billing) and to select **Use this card**. Then delete your card. The Management API has no endpoint for payment methods. A payment method that stays with the organization can still be charged after you leave.
5. Remove yourself with the [remove organization members](/docs/reference/api/v2-remove-organization-members) endpoint. Use your own `id` from the list of members. The request fails while you're the only Owner.

   ```bash
   export MEMBER_ID="your-member-id"

   curl -X DELETE "https://api.supabase.com/v2/organizations/$ORG_SLUG/members/$MEMBER_ID" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN"
   ```

</TabPanel>
</Tabs>

If the organization's billing email is your address, it changes to a remaining Owner's address when you leave. A billing email set to any other address stays the same.

For more information, see [Transferring ownership of an organization](/docs/guides/platform/access-control#transferring-ownership-of-an-organization).

### Recover access to the Owner account

Only an Owner of the source organization can transfer a project. Supabase support can't transfer a project for you and doesn't decide ownership disputes.

If you can't sign in to the Owner account, [contact support](https://supabase.help). Support must verify that you own the account before it restores access, and it usually relies on billing records to do so. An organization on the Free Plan has no billing records, so support might not be able to restore access.

### Vercel Marketplace

You can transfer a project out of an organization managed through the [Vercel Marketplace](/docs/guides/integrations/vercel-marketplace), or into one. To transfer a project out, you must be an Owner of the Vercel-managed organization.

<Admonition type="danger">

Uninstalling the Vercel Marketplace integration deletes every project still in the Vercel-managed organization. Transfer every project you want to keep before you uninstall the integration.

</Admonition>

To move projects from a Vercel-managed organization to a Supabase-managed organization:

1. [Transfer each project](#transfer-a-project). The transfer removes the linked storage resource from your Vercel project.
2. On the target organization's [**Integrations**](/dashboard/org/_/integrations) page, click **Install Vercel integration**, then click **Add new project connection** for each project. Reconnect the integration to restore environment variables.
3. After every project is out of the Vercel-managed organization, uninstall the Marketplace integration in Vercel.

### Other partner-managed projects

For other partners, start with the following:

- **Lovable.** An app built with Lovable uses either Lovable Cloud or your own Supabase project as its backend. To tell the two apart, see [Identifying Lovable backend: Lovable Cloud or Supabase](/kb/troubleshooting/identify-lovable-cloud-or-supabase-backend).
- **AWS Marketplace.** AWS billing is linked to an organization, and you can link an organization you already have. You don't need to transfer projects. See [AWS Marketplace account setup](/docs/guides/platform/aws-marketplace/account-setup).

### Stripe Projects

A project provisioned through Stripe Projects can't be transferred while it's linked to Stripe.

<Admonition type="danger">

Removing the project's resource in Stripe deletes the Supabase project and its data. The Dashboard message asks you to deprovision the project through Stripe before you transfer it. Back up your data before you remove anything in Stripe.

</Admonition>

To move the data to another organization, create a project in that organization and copy the data to it. See [Migrating within Supabase](/docs/guides/platform/migrating-within-supabase).

### Transfer to a different region

A transfer moves a project between organizations. It can't move a project to a different region. To change the region, see [Migrating within Supabase](/docs/guides/platform/migrating-within-supabase).

## Troubleshooting

When a check fails, the Dashboard shows a **Project cannot be transferred** box with one or more messages. The Management API's preview endpoint returns the same messages in its `errors` list. The transfer endpoint returns HTTP `400` with the messages after the text `Transfer is not possible, due to the following reasons:`.

The following tables list each message with its cause and fix. Some long messages are shortened to their first sentences, so match on the start of the message.

### Role and membership messages

The following table lists the messages about your role in the two organizations:

| Message                                                                                      | Cause                                                                                                                                                  | Fix                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| You must be an owner of the project to transfer a project.                                   | You aren't an Owner of the source organization.                                                                                                        | Ask an Owner to transfer the project or to make you an Owner.                                           |
| You must be a member of the target organization to transfer a project.                       | The account you're signed in with isn't a member of the target organization. An SSO account counts as a separate account.                              | Ask for an invitation for this account.                                                                 |
| You must have organization permissions within the target organization to transfer a project. | You have the **No access** role or only project-scoped roles in the target organization, or your access token doesn't include the target organization. | Ask an Owner for a role for the whole organization, or create a token that includes both organizations. |

### The transfer button is missing or disabled

- **The Transfer project section is missing.** You're viewing a branch. Open the main project instead.
- **Transfer project is disabled.** The tooltip gives the reason:
  - `You need additional permissions to transfer this project`: only an Owner of the source organization can transfer.
  - `Project transfers are temporarily disabled, please try again later.`: transfers are paused for all projects.
- **The dialog shows `You do not have any organizations you can transfer your project to.`** Your account is a member of no other organization. Create an organization, or ask for an invitation to one.
- **Transfer Project in the dialog is disabled.** No target organization is selected, the checks are still running, or a check is failing. Read the **Project cannot be transferred** box.

### The transfer fails after you confirm

If you see a message that starts with `Failed to transfer project:` after you confirm, compare the rest of the message with the messages in the preceding tables. If the text matches none of them, check which organization owns the project before you try again.
