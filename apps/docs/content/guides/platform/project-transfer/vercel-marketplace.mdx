Move a project out of an organization managed through the [Vercel Marketplace](/docs/guides/integrations/vercel-marketplace) and into a Supabase-managed organization. To transfer a project into a Vercel-managed organization, use the steps in [Transfer a project](/docs/guides/platform/project-transfer) with that organization as the target.

## Prerequisites

Before you move a project out of a Vercel-managed organization, check that you meet the following requirements:

- You're an **Owner** of the Vercel-managed organization. Your role comes from your Vercel team. See [How your Vercel team maps to Supabase](/docs/guides/integrations/vercel-marketplace#how-your-vercel-team-maps-to-supabase).
- You meet the other [project transfer requirements](/docs/guides/platform/project-transfer/requirements), including a role for the whole organization in the target organization.

## Procedure

To move projects from a Vercel-managed organization to a Supabase-managed organization, follow these steps:

1. Transfer each project, with the steps in [Transfer a project](/docs/guides/platform/project-transfer). The transfer removes the linked storage resource from your Vercel project, and the project stops accepting tokens that Vercel issued for your team.
2. On the target organization's [**Integrations**](/dashboard/org/_/integrations) page, click **Install Vercel integration**, then click **Add new project connection** for each project. Reconnect the integration to restore environment variables. The Management API has no endpoint for the Vercel integration, so use the Dashboard for this step.
3. After every project is out of the Vercel-managed organization, uninstall the Marketplace integration in Vercel.

   <Admonition type="danger">

   Uninstalling the Vercel Marketplace integration deletes every project still in the Vercel-managed organization. Transfer every project you want to keep before you uninstall the integration.

   </Admonition>
