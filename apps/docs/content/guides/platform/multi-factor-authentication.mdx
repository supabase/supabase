<Admonition type="note">

This guide is for adding MFA to your Supabase user account. If you want to enable MFA for users in your Supabase project, refer to [this guide](/docs/guides/auth/auth-mfa) instead.

</Admonition>

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adds an additional layer of security to your user account, by requiring a second factor to verify your user identity. Supabase allows users to enable MFA on their account and set it as a requirement for subsequent logins.

## Supported authentication factors

Currently, Supabase supports adding a unique time-based one-time password (TOTP) to your user account as an additional security factor. You can manage your TOTP factor using apps such as 1Password, Authy, Google Authenticator or Apple's Keychain.

## Enable MFA

You can enable MFA for your user account under your [Supabase account settings](/dashboard/account/security). Enabling MFA will result in all other [user sessions](/docs/guides/auth/sessions) to be automatically logged out and forced to sign in again with MFA.

<Admonition type="caution">

For security reasons, we will not be able to restore access to your account if you lose all your two-factor authentication credentials. We recommend you register a backup factor.

</Admonition>

## Sign in with MFA

Once you've enabled MFA for your Supabase user account, you will be prompted to enter your second factor challenge code as seen in your preferred TOTP app.

If you are an organization owner and on the Pro, Team or Enterprise plan, you can enforce that all organization members [must have MFA enabled](/docs/guides/platform/mfa/org-mfa-enforcement).

## Recovery codes

Recovery codes let you complete the MFA step if you lose access to your authenticator app. You get 10 codes, and each code works once. Store them somewhere safe, such as a password manager.

When you enable MFA, the Supabase Dashboard generates your recovery codes and shows them once. Copy or download them before you close the dialog.

### Sign in with a recovery code

If you can't use your authenticator app, sign in with a recovery code:

1. Sign in with your usual method.
2. On the two-factor authentication screen, under **Unable to sign in?**, click **Authenticate using a recovery code**.
3. Enter one of your recovery codes.

After 5 incorrect codes, sign-in with a recovery code is locked for 15 minutes.

If you lost your authenticator app, go to **Account → Security → Multi-factor authentication**. Select **Add MFA method** and verify the new authenticator. Then select **Remove MFA method** for the lost authenticator. Removing your last MFA method disables MFA and deletes your recovery codes.”```

### Generate or regenerate recovery codes

Your account security settings show how many recovery codes you have left. If your account has MFA but no recovery codes, click **Generate recovery codes**. To replace your codes with a new set, click **Regenerate**. Your previous codes stop working immediately.

## Disable MFA

You can disable MFA for your user account under your [Supabase account settings](/dashboard/account/security). On subsequent sign-in attempts, you will not be prompted to enter an MFA code. Disabling MFA also deletes your recovery codes.

<Admonition type="caution">

We strongly recommend that you do not disable MFA to avoid unauthorized access to your user account.

</Admonition>
