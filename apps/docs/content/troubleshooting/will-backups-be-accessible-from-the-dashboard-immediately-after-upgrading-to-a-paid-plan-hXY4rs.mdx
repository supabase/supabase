The documented daily-backup guarantee applies to paid projects. Pro projects can access the last seven days of daily backups after those backups have been generated and retained.

Do not rely on an upgrade revealing historical backups from the project's Free Plan period. Check [Database > Backups](/dashboard/project/_/database/backups/scheduled) after upgrading, and maintain your own exports while a project is on the Free Plan. See [Database backups](/docs/guides/platform/backups) for current retention by plan.
