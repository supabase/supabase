---
title: Can I use a third-party authentication provider and Supabase Auth in the same project?
topics: ['Auth']
---

Yes. You can use a third-party authentication provider alongside [Supabase Auth](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth), or on its own. Supabase Auth stays enabled in every project, but your users do not need to sign in with it.

These third-party providers can then access Supabase features (like the [Data API (REST and GraphQL)](/docs/guides/database/overview), [Storage](/docs/guides/storage), [Realtime](/docs/guides/realtime) and [Edge functions](/docs/guides/functions)) from your existing apps.

For more details how this affects pricing, refer to [Manage Monthly Active Third-Party Users usage](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/monthly-active-users-third-party).
