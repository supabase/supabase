---
title: Can I use a third-party provider and Supabase Auth in the same project?
---

Yes. You can use a provider alongside Supabase Auth, or on its own. Supabase Auth stays enabled in every project at this time, but your users don't need to sign in with it.

For more details how this affects pricing, refer to [Manage Monthly Active Third-Party Users usage](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/monthly-active-users-third-party).
