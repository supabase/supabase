---
title: How do I run scheduled tasks (cron-style) in my app?
topics: ['Database']
lastEdited: 2026-10-28
---

Use [Supabase Cron](/docs/guides/cron). It simplifies scheduling recurring jobs with cron syntax and monitoring job runs inside Postgres.

Cron Jobs can be created via SQL or the Dashboard, and can run anywhere from every second to once a year depending on your use case.

Follow the [Cron quickstart](/docs/guides/cron/quickstart) for a complete example.
