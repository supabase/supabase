---
title: Can I connect to Supabase Postgres from serverless and edge functions?
topics: ['Database']
lastEdited: 2026-10-27
---

Yes, though the connection methods vary:

- For Vercel Edge functions, follow the guidelines in the [Supabase docs](/docs/guides/database/connecting-to-postgres/serverless-drivers#vercel-edge-functions)
- For Cloudflare Workers, follow the [Cloudflare's guidelines](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/databases/third-party-integrations/supabase/) to set up Supabase.
- For other serverless providers, connect through the [shared pooler](/docs/guides/database/connecting-to-postgres#pooler-transaction-mode) rather than directly. Serverless platforms open many short-lived connections to your Postgres instance, which can overload the available connections.

For more detailed guidance, see [Serverless drivers](/docs/guides/database/connecting-to-postgres/serverless-drivers).
