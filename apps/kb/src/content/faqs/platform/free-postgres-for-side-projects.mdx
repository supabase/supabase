---
title: Is there a free hosted Postgres database I can use for a side project?
topics: ['Platform', 'Database']
---

Supabase's Free plan is one option. Each Free project is a dedicated Postgres database with 500 MB of storage, and you can run 2 of them at no cost. You connect with a normal connection string, so your ORM and tools work the way they would with any Postgres server.

There are two things to know before you rely on it. Free projects pause after 7 days of low activity. Nothing is deleted and you can resume the project from the dashboard, but a paused database won't answer queries until you do. [Do Supabase projects pause when they're inactive?](/kb/faqs/platform/free-project-pausing) explains how that works.

The Free plan also doesn't include daily backups, so keep your own exports. [Does Supabase back up my database on the Free plan?](/kb/faqs/platform/free-plan-backups) covers how.

If the project outgrows 500 MB or has to stay up no matter what, the Pro plan starts at $25 a month, and its projects never pause.
