---
title: How much does a database-only Supabase project cost?
topics: ['Platform', 'Database']
---

Nothing, if it fits in the Free plan. A Free project gets a 500 MB database on shared CPU with 500 MB of RAM, and you can have 2 active Free projects. The catch is that Free projects pause after a week of low activity.

On the Pro plan, one database-only project costs $25 a month before any extra usage. That includes $10 a month in compute credits, which covers a project on Micro compute (1 GB of RAM), along with 8 GB of disk, 250 GB of egress, daily backups kept for 7 days, and email support. Pro projects don't pause.

The bill grows in a few predictable ways. Each extra project runs on its own compute instance, billed by the hour. Larger compute sizes cost more. Disk past 8 GB is charged per extra GB, and add-ons like Point-in-Time Recovery are priced separately.

The [pricing page](https://supabase.com/pricing) has the current numbers, and [Compute and disk](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/compute-and-disk) lists every compute size.
