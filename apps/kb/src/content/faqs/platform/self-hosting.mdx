---
title: Can I self-host Supabase?
topics: ['Platform']
---

Yes. Supabase is open source, and you can run it on your own servers with [Docker](/docs/guides/self-hosting/docker). It's a good choice when you need full control over where your data lives, when compliance rules prevent you from using a managed service, or when Supabase has to run in an isolated network.

Self-hosted Supabase isn't identical to the managed platform. It runs a single project, most settings are configured through environment variables instead of the dashboard, and some platform features, such as managed backups, aren't available. For more details, see [Self-hosting](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/self-hosting#how-self-hosted-supabase-differs).

If you want Supabase on your own machine for development, you don't need to self-host at all. The [Supabase CLI](/docs/guides/local-development/cli-workflows) runs the whole stack locally.
