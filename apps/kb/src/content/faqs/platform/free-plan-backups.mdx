---
title: Does Supabase automatically back up my database on the Free plan?
topics: ['Platform', 'Database']
---

No, but you can perform backups manually through the [Supabase CLI](/docs/guides/platform/migrating-within-supabase/backup-restore).

For automatic daily backups, upgrade to the Pro plan or above. Supabase also offers [Point-in-Time Recovery](/docs/guides/platform/backups#point-in-time-recovery) as an add-on for paid plans, which lets you restore to an exact moment instead of the last daily backup.
