---
title: Does Supabase back up my database on the Free plan?
topics: ['Platform', 'Database']
---

No. Daily backups start on the Pro plan, which keeps the last 7 days of them. Team keeps 14 days and Enterprise up to 30. On the Free plan, you're responsible for your own exports.

The Supabase CLI can export everything, but it takes three separate dumps: one for your custom roles, one for the schema, and one for the data. Running `supabase db dump` with no flags exports only the schema, so a backup made that way restores empty tables. The [backup and restore guide](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/migrating-within-supabase/backup-restore) has the exact commands and shows how to restore them into a new project.

Those dumps don't include files you've uploaded to Storage, since the database only holds their metadata, so back those up separately. If you've changed the Supabase-managed `auth` or `storage` schemas, for example with your own triggers or policies, the guide covers restoring those changes too.

If you'd rather restore to an exact moment than to the last daily backup, Point-in-Time Recovery is available as an add-on on paid plans. See [Database backups](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups).
