---
title: Do Supabase projects pause when they're inactive?
topics: ['Platform']
lastEdited: 2026-10-27
---

Only on the Free plan. If a Free project gets too little database activity over a week, Supabase pauses it. Projects on other plans (Pro, Team, and Enterprise) are never paused for inactivity.

You get an email about a week before a pause, and another once it happens. Opening the project in the dashboard or sending real requests from your app both count as activity. A few requests a day is usually enough to keep a project running, though that isn't a guarantee.

A paused project keeps all of its data and settings. To bring it back, see [Restoring a paused project](/docs/guides/platform/free-project-pausing#restoring-a-paused-project).

If people rely on what you're building, even with light traffic, [upgrade the project to a paid plan](/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-subscription#upgrade).
