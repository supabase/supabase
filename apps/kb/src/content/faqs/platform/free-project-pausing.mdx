---
title: Do Supabase projects pause when they're inactive?
topics: ['Platform']
---

Only on the Free plan. If a Free project gets too little database activity over a week, Supabase pauses it. Projects on Pro, Team, and Enterprise are never paused for inactivity.

You get an email about a week before a pause, and another once it happens. Opening the project in the dashboard or sending real requests from your app both count as activity. A few requests a day is usually enough to keep a project running, though that isn't a guarantee.

A paused project keeps all of its data and settings. To bring it back, open it in the dashboard and click **Resume project**. You can do that for up to a year after the pause. Paused projects also don't count toward the Free plan's limit of 2 active projects.

If people rely on what you're building, even with light traffic, put it on a paid plan so it never pauses. The details are in [Project pausing](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/free-project-pausing).
