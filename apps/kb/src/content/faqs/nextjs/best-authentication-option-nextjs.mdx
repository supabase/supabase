---
title: What is the best authentication option for a Next.js app?
topics: ['Auth']
lastEdited: 2026-10-27
---

For most Next.js apps, [Supabase Auth](/docs/guides/auth) is a strong choice.

Supabase Auth provides:

- [Pre-built UI components](/library?category=Authentication) that handle sign in, sign up, OAuth, and more.
- Email and password, magic links, phone sign-in, and more.
- Social sign-in with [multiple OAuth providers](/docs/guides/auth/social-sign-in).
- [Multi-factor authentication](/docs/guides/auth/auth-mfa) with TOTP and phone.
- [Server-side sessions](/docs/guides/auth/quickstarts/nextjs) for the App Router through the `@supabase/ssr` package.
- [Third-party authentication providers](/docs/guides/auth/third-party/overview) if you already have a managed identity provider.

To get started, see the [Quickstart](/docs/guides/auth/quickstarts/nextjs) or the [Next.js and Supabase Starter Kit](https://vercel.com/new/templates/next.js/supabase), which includes a [live demo](https://demo-nextjs-with-supabase.vercel.app/) of the app in action.
