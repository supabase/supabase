---
title: How do I add authentication to a Next.js app?
topics: ['Auth']
---

Use Supabase Auth with the `@supabase/ssr` package to [add authentication to a Next.js app](/docs/guides/auth/quickstarts/nextjs). It handles sign-up, sign-in, sessions, and social login, and it works with the Next.js App Router's server components and route handlers.

To see a full flow end to end - a demo app where users can sign up or sign in and then update their account with public profile information - follow [Build a User Management App with Next.js](/docs/guides/getting-started/tutorials/with-nextjs) or use the [Supabase Starter](https://vercel.com/new/templates/next.js/supabase) from the Vercel template library.
