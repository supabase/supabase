title: 'Will backups be accessible from the dashboard immediately after upgrading to a paid plan?'
description: 'Confirms that daily backups accumulated on the free plan become available in the dashboard as soon as a project upgrades to a paid plan.'
topics: ['Troubleshooting', 'Supabase Platform', 'Database']
github_url: 'https://github.com/orgs/supabase/discussions/22713'

We are currently taking up to 7 daily backups that will be available for you once you upgrade. Keep in mind that we might no longer make daily backups for free projects in the future.

But at the moment, you'll be able to access them after upgrading.
